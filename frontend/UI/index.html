About Us

Our Proud History

Chandigarh College of Engineering and Technology, Sector -26, Chandigarh (CCET) formerly known as Central Polytechnic Chandigarh (CPC), was established in 1959. To cater to the aspirations and ambitions of the exponentially growing population of Chandigarh, the Chandigarh Administration upgraded CPC to CCET by introducing two branches of engineering in 2002.

1959

Founded as Central Polytechnic Chandigarh (CPC)

2002

Upgraded to Chandigarh College of Engineering & Technology

2010

Awarded "Best Polytechnic" in Northern Region

2020

State-of-the-art research labs established

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote excellence in technical education and related areas of social research through high quality teaching, innovative courses and skill enhancement to transform students into committed and responsible technocrats in a challenging world.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a premier institution of technical education that nurtures talent, fosters innovation, and contributes to the advancement of society through cutting-edge research and ethical leadership.
